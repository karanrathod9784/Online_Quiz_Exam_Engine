About Our Project

An Online Quiz and Exam Engine designed to make examinations simple, organized and accessible. Test your knowledge and track your performance through an easy-to-use platform.

Who We Are

The Online Quiz and Exam Engine is a web-based application developed using Python and Flask. It provides a platform where students can register, log in, attempt online multiple-choice quizzes and view their examination results.


Administrators can create quizzes, add questions, manage examinations and monitor student performance. The system aims to make online assessments more efficient and user-friendly.

Project Objectives

Easy Online Examinations

Allow students to attempt quizzes online without requiring paper-based examinations.

Automated Evaluation

Calculate quiz scores automatically and provide students with their results.

Performance Tracking

Maintain student examination history and provide performance analytics.

Quiz Management

Provide administrators with tools to create quizzes, manage questions and monitor results.

Technologies Used

Python Flask SQLite HTML5 CSS3 JavaScript Matplotlib
